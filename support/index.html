Common questions

I can’t hear the numbers

Check that announcements aren’t muted in the app and that your device volume is up. Announcements use your device’s text-to-speech service; on Android, make sure a speech engine with an English voice is installed in your device settings.

What does Reset Game do?

It clears the called numbers and marks every prize as unclaimed, so you can start a new game. Your prize list and settings stay. It does not delete everything the app has saved; see Deleting your information.

Will my game still be there if I close the app?

Yes. The game and prizes are saved on your device and come back when you reopen the app. If they can’t be read, the app tells you instead of starting over silently.

Is Tambola Adda a gambling app?

No. Tambola Adda is a number-calling and record-keeping tool for in-person games. It does not promote or encourage gambling, accept bets, collect entry fees, process payments or pay out prizes. Prize amounts are records entered by the host.

Does the app need an account or internet?

No. There is no account, and the game works without an internet connection. Links to this website and to email open in your browser or email app.
