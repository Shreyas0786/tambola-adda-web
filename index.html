A companion for Tambola hosts

Tambola Adda helps the host of an in-person Tambola or Housie game call numbers from 1 to 90, announce them aloud and keep track of prizes. It has no accounts, ads or online play.

Tambola Adda is a number-calling and record-keeping tool for in-person games. It does not promote or encourage gambling, accept bets, collect entry fees, process payments or pay out prizes. Prize amounts are records entered by the host.
