How it works

You host. Tambola Adda keeps count.

  1. 1

    Call it out loud

    Tap Call Next. Your phone announces each number from 1 to 90 with its traditional phrase, or stays quiet when muted.

  2. 2

    Keep the board in view

    Called numbers are highlighted on the 1–90 board, and recent calls are listed under the current number.

  3. 3

    Tick off the prizes

    Set up Early Five, lines and Full House, then mark each one claimed as it is won.

Prizes

Know what’s claimed and what’s left

Keep every prize and amount in one list. The game summary shows the pool, what has been claimed and how far the game has got.

Next round? Reset Game clears the called numbers and unclaims every prize.

Themes

Bright room or late night

Choose light or dark, or follow your phone’s setting. In the dark theme, called numbers are outlined in orange.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I get Tambola Adda?

Tambola Adda is available now for iPhone. Download Tambola Adda on the App Store.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Calling numbers, the board and prize tracking all work without an internet connection. Links to this website and to email open in your browser or email app.

Do I need an account? Are there ads?

No. There is no account to create, and the app has no ads or analytics.

Is it a Housie caller too?

Yes. Tambola and Housie are two names for the same game played with numbers from 1 to 90, so Tambola Adda works as a Housie caller as well.

Can players join online?

No. Tambola Adda is for hosting in-person games and has no online play.

What happens if I close the app mid-game?

The game and prizes are saved on your device and come back when you reopen the app.

Is Tambola Adda a gambling app?

No. Tambola Adda is a number-calling and record-keeping tool for in-person games. It does not promote or encourage gambling, accept bets, collect entry fees, process payments or pay out prizes. Prize amounts are records entered by the host.
